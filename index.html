About Us

Healthcare Love is an appointment scheduling and healthcare support platform that helps patients connect with licensed healthcare professionals. We provide secure scheduling, administrative support, and access to healthcare services across the United States. Healthcare Love does NOT provide medical care. All medical care and advice are provided solely by independent, licensed professionals.

Important Operational Information

Healthcare Love operates as an appointment scheduling and administrative support platform for licensed healthcare professionals.


We do not sell pharmaceuticals, prescribe medications directly, dispense controlled substances, or provide emergency medical services.


Payments processed through our platform are for appointment scheduling, consultation fees, administrative support services, and related healthcare services.


In case of a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.
